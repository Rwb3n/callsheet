## Summary

Analysis of seven analogous B2B directory and marketplace platforms confirms CALLSHEET's proposed £199 / £399 / £699 annual pricing sits in the defensible middle of the market. The pure directory-listing subscription range across comparable platforms is £120–£960/year. CALLSHEET captures ~85% of that viable range while avoiding the extremes.

**This is the third and final piece of the pricing puzzle.** Combined with the competitor pricing research (UK production directories) and the freemium conversion research (tier structure and conversion mechanics), the pricing hypothesis can now be evaluated from three angles.

**Refinement flagged:** The £199→£399 gap is a 100% increase. Research notes some platforms (Checkatrade, LinkedIn) use smaller increments at the low end. A possible intermediate step (e.g., £149 entry or £299 mid) could capture more sole traders. However, the existing tiering is standard for UK B2B SaaS and not a dealbreaker.

## Key Caveats

- **Pricing opacity is the norm.** 5 of 7 platforms don't fully publish pricing. Transparent, published pricing is itself a competitive advantage for CALLSHEET.
- **All pricing pre-VAT unless stated.** CALLSHEET must clearly state VAT treatment — £199 ex-VAT = £239 inc-VAT. 20% difference in perceived price.
- **Niche dynamics cut both ways.** Smaller addressable market (downward pressure) but less competition and higher willingness-to-pay for targeted visibility (upward pressure).
- **Clutch is USD.** $499/yr ≈ £399 at current rates. ±10% FX sensitivity.

## PRICING TENSION RESOLUTION

All three commercial research streams are now complete. Here's the synthesis:

### The Tension

| Research Stream | Tier 1 Proposal | Tier 2 Proposal | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Freemium conversion** | £29-49/mo (£348-588/yr) | £79-129/mo (£948-1,548/yr) | B2B SaaS norms (Clutch, LinkedIn, Yelp advertising) |
| **UK competitor pricing** | £199/yr (£19/mo) | £399/yr (£39/mo) | What UK production directories actually charge |
| **Analogous directory pricing** | £199/yr confirmed | £399/yr confirmed | Cross-market B2B directory band (£120-960/yr) |

The freemium research priced from the SaaS/platform world. The competitor and analogous research both independently converged on £199/£399/£699. Two out of three streams agree.

### Resolution

**£199 / £399 / £699 annual is the pricing.** Rationale:

1. **Market anchor is stronger than SaaS anchor.** UK production providers currently pay 4rfv £120-395/yr and The Knowledge £495-1,120/yr. They think in annual terms, not monthly subscriptions. A post-house finance team processing "£399/yr for a directory listing" is routine; "£39/month SaaS subscription" triggers different procurement psychology.

2. **Two independent research streams converged.** The competitor pricing research (anchored on UK production market) and the analogous pricing research (anchored on cross-market B2B directories) both landed on £199/£399/£699 without coordination. This is signal.

3. **The freemium research is still valid — but for the conversion mechanics, not the pricing.** The tier structure (free → visibility amplification → competitive intelligence), the analytics-as-conversion-lever playbook, the anti-patterns, the activation triggers — all hold. The monthly price points don't, because they were benchmarked against platforms in different markets with different buyer psychology.

4. **Monthly option as premium.** Offer £19/mo / £39/mo / £69/mo for providers who want flexibility, but present annual as default with the implicit ~15% discount that annual pricing represents. This is standard across all platforms studied.

### Confirmed Pricing Architecture

| Tier | Annual | Monthly | Target Persona |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | £0 | £0 | Directory population. Buyer-satisfying base. |
| **Professional** | **£199/yr** | £19/mo | Sole traders, emerging freelancers |
| **Premium** | **£399/yr** | £39/mo | Established freelancers, small companies |
| **Partner** | **£699/yr** | £69/mo | Established companies, facilities, post houses |

**Launch discount:** First year Professional at £99 (matches Mandy's price point, reduces switching cost to near-zero).
