## Summary

The UK production directory market generates £15-20M annually across all platforms but is dominated by legacy incumbents charging £120-£1,120/year on outdated technology. No competitor offers analytics, verification, self-service activation, or modern matching at any price. The £150-400/year zone is underserved for feature-rich B2B listings. CALLSHEET has a clear pricing window.

## Universal Feature Gating Patterns

Three features are gated behind payment across ALL platforms studied:

1. **Logo/headshot display** — free listings show no visual identity
2. **Extended description text** — free listings have no or minimal text
3. **Search priority/placement** — free listings rank last

These represent the minimum viable upgrade trigger for any directory.

**No UK competitor offers at any price:** Profile analytics, lead tracking, enquiry attribution, competitor benchmarking, verification/trust badges (beyond Mandy's basic credit check), self-service instant activation.

## Pricing Gaps Identified

| Gap | Opportunity |
|---|---|
| £150-400/yr zone underserved for feature-rich B2B listings | 4rfv Basic (£120) = barely functional. 4rfv Profile (£395) = reasonable but dated. Knowledge doesn't offer meaningful paid tier below £495. |
| No analytics at any price | Only Mandy offers basic "who viewed" — and only for individuals, not B2B. Standard in modern SaaS, absent from this market. |
| No verification at any price | No trust badges, verified reviews, insurance verification, BECTU indicators, or quality certifications on any UK platform. |
| No self-service activation anywhere | Every incumbent requires manual contact, phone, or email to upgrade. |
| No combined directory + matching + intelligence | The Knowledge sells Production Intelligence separately at £545/yr. 4rfv separates news from directory. Mandy's job board disconnected from directory. |

**Willingness-to-pay ceiling:** ~£500-600/yr for standard suppliers, based on 4rfv Profile (£395) and Knowledge Enhanced (£495) as most commonly purchased tiers. Knowledge Premier (£1,120) is likely large-supplier-only.

## Pricing Recommendations from Research

The agent recommended:

| Tier | Price | Positioning |
|---|---|---|
| Free ("Listed") | £0 | Company name, basic contact, 1 category, 50-word description, organic search |
| Tier 1 ("Professional") | £199/yr (£19/mo) | 60% cheaper than Knowledge Enhanced. Logo, 300 words, 10 images, 1 showreel, 3 categories, basic analytics, verified badge |
| Tier 2 ("Premium") | £399/yr (£39/mo) | Sits between 4rfv Profile (£395) and Knowledge Enhanced (£495). Unlimited media, 5 showreels, 10 categories, priority search, advanced analytics, insurance verification |
| Tier 3 ("Partner") | £699/yr (£69/mo) | Below Knowledge Premier (£1,120). Account manager, custom branding, multi-user, production intelligence alerts |

**Launch discount suggestion:** First year Professional at £99 (matches Mandy's annual price — psychologically familiar).

## Decisions Confirmed by This Research

| Decision | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Buyers search free, providers pay | **CONFIRMED** | Universal across all 6+ platforms studied. Angie's List proved buyer friction fatal. |
| Search ranking is the core monetisation lever | **CONFIRMED** | The Knowledge's 4-tier hierarchy, 4rfv's sponsored category, ProductionHub's featured listings — all gate placement behind payment. |
| Free tier must include contact details for buyers | **CONFIRMED** | 4rfv Basic (£120) adds contact links as its only paid feature. Contact gating is standard at the lowest paid tier. CALLSHEET should give this away free per freemium research anti-patterns. |
| Self-service, published pricing is a differentiator | **CONFIRMED** | No UK competitor offers this. All require manual contact. |
| Analytics is a differentiator no competitor offers | **CONFIRMED** | Only Mandy has basic "who viewed" for individuals. Zero B2B analytics anywhere. |
| Advertising is a separate revenue stream | **CONFIRMED** | 4rfv rate card: £290-600/unit. The Knowledge: display + print. ProductionHub: job posts $75-165. |

## Flagged Risks

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| 4rfv's SEO authority (claims 70% top-10 Google rankings) | High | CALLSHEET cannot compete on SEO at launch. Initial buyer acquisition must be direct (industry partnerships, production manager outreach, trade show presence). SEO is a medium-term play. |
| The Knowledge's heritage and industry trust | Medium | "Industry bible since 1986" carries weight. CALLSHEET's value prop is features/price, not heritage. Must deliver measurably better outcomes (more enquiries, faster matching). |
| Market inertia — providers paying 4rfv out of habit | Medium | Launch discount (£99/yr) reduces switching cost to nearly zero. "Claim your listing" flow for providers already in enriched database lowers friction. |
| Kays at £48 one-off sets a low anchor | Low | Different model (print + digital, no tiers, no analytics). But some providers may compare and balk at £199/yr. Mitigate with genuinely differentiated features Kays can't match. |
| 4rfv's 12,500 vs our 4,700 figure discrepancy | Medium | 4rfv likely counts all historical listings including inactive/duplicate. Need to verify through scraping whether 12,500 are genuinely active. If so, addressable market is larger than modelled. |
