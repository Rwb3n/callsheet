# CALLSHEET — Strategic Positioning

**Status:** Locked  
**Last updated:** 2026-02-10

---

## One-liner

A modern, actively-maintained B2B discovery and matching platform for production services, launching in broadcast/film/TV. Flat-fee listings with enriched profiles and intelligent matching. Designed to be vertical-extensible.

## Problem Statement

The incumbent (4rfv.co.uk) operates as a passive phonebook. Static listings, no enrichment, dead-end profile pages, no matchmaking. Data decays and nobody maintains it. Provider pages are dead ends — users land and don't know where to go next.

## Timing Thesis

The production industry is at an inflection point. Virtual production, AI tooling, and shifting economics are creating new entrants — companies and freelancers who don't have established networks and can't rely on "who you know." They need a platform to find and be found.

## Target Customer

**Paying customer:** Service providers — studios, freelancers, equipment hire, post-production houses, facilities, and other B2B production services.

**Platform user:** Buyers of production services — producers, production managers, commissioners, agencies.

## Revenue Model (V1)

Flat-fee subscription. Tiered listings (free basic → paid enhanced/priority). Chosen for operational simplicity and predictable revenue. Pay-per-introduction may be layered in once usage data exists to define and price an "introduction."

## Competitive Advantage

- Active data enrichment vs passive directory
- Intelligent matching vs manual browsing
- Modern UX vs legacy interface
- Clean, maintained taxonomy vs decaying categories

## Scope

- **V1:** Broadcast / Film / TV production services (UK & Ireland)
- **V2+:** New verticals (gaming, events, digital content production)
- **Architecture:** Vertical-agnostic from day one at the data model and platform level

## Incumbent Analysis

| Attribute | 4rfv.co.uk | CALLSHEET |
|---|---|---|
| Listings | ~4,700 (scraped) | Derived + enriched |
| Data freshness | Passive, decaying | Actively maintained |
| Matching | None | Intelligent, attribute-based |
| UX | Legacy, desktop-centric | Modern, search-first, mobile |
| Revenue | Listings fees + ads | Flat-fee subscription |
| Content | Trade news + directory | Platform-first (content is V1.5) |
| Age | 15+ years | Greenfield |
